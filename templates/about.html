⚠️ Important Limitations

  • The model is evaluated on PlantVillage lab-controlled images. Real-world field photos with different lighting, backgrounds, or overlapping symptoms may perform differently.
  • Classification is limited to the {{ num_classes }} verified PlantVillage labels. Images outside those labels will still be assigned one of the known classes.
  • MobileNetV3-Large uses ImageNet pretrained weights and is fine-tuned on PlantVillage. This is transfer learning and does not guarantee field performance.
  • Confidence is the model's class probability, not disease severity, clinical certainty, or a calibrated field risk score.
  • Do not use for actual crop management decisions without consulting a qualified agronomist.

Model & inference

Architecture: torchvision MobileNetV3-Large with a replaced {{ num_classes }}-class classifier head.
Initialization: ImageNet pretrained weights, then fine-tuned on PlantVillage (transfer learning).
Preprocessing: 224 × 224 crop and ImageNet channel normalization.
Inference: Flask validates the upload, runs PyTorch on CUDA where available (CPU fallback), maps the output index, and retrieves class guidance.
Persistence: SQLite records the filename, predicted class, probability, top predictions, model, and guidance.
Evaluation: best checkpoint is selected by validation macro-F1; the held-out test split is evaluated separately.
